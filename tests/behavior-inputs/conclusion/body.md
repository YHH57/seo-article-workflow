# Understanding Coffee Grind Size

A grind setting is a starting point for a brewing recipe. Water moves through a bed of ground coffee, so changing the grind can change how a brew behaves. The same numbered setting does not identify the same result on every grinder.

## Begin with the brewing method

Use a starting range suited to the method and the grinder's own guidance. A filter recipe and an immersion recipe may call for different settings. A setting suggested for one recipe is a reference point rather than a universal answer.

## Change one thing at a time

When testing a grind adjustment, keep the coffee dose, water amount and other recipe choices stable. Note what changed in the brew and whether you preferred the result. Changing several recipe choices together makes it harder to understand the effect of the grind adjustment.

## Keep a useful record

Record the grinder, setting, recipe and your observations. This gives the next adjustment a known starting point. The aim is a repeatable approach that suits the coffee and recipe you use, not a universal numbered setting.
